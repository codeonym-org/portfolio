# 004 — Erebus is a cavern, dark and fogged, and nothing in it glows like a toy

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Taken by:** the owner (direction), the agent (choices)

The owner rejected the sealed hall of decision 003 as childish: neon veins
in a polished floor, saturated purple light, cartoon ruins. He asked for the
inside of a dungeon, a cave or a maze, in the mood of a Skyrim cave, a
different floor, and a dark, foggy, mysterious planet for Erebus in the Gate
Realm. This replaces the setting of decision 003; the statues, the colossus,
the door and the rules of the fight stay.

- **Setting:** a cavern the rock closed over, with the ruin left inside it.
  The wall, the roof, the columns, the drip stone and the boulders are built
  from noise when the gate opens (`cavern.tsx`); no model is involved. The
  floor is bare rock and grit, flat where the fight is, heaped toward the
  wall. Textures: Dark Rock 02 and Rock Ground (Poly Haven, CC0).
- **Light:** one cold light falling through a break in the roof, four iron
  braziers with a shader flame, a faint lamp on the hunter. Exponential fog,
  mist lying on the floor, dust in the air. Film grain, less saturation, less
  bloom.
- **No neon:** the glowing cracks under the floor are gone. Obelisks, blades,
  pickups, beacons and rings keep their meaning but lose most of their glow
  and saturation. What the System does to the world now shows in the colour
  of the light from the roof, and a blackout still puts every light out.
- **The hunter** wears black iron and carries the one clean steel blade, so
  it reads apart from the stone statues. The golem lost its blue texture.
- **Removed:** the stylized pillar and rubble models and the monastery floor
  texture.
- **Not a maze:** the simulation is a round floor with a ring of obelisks.
  Corridors would need walls the simulation knows about (collision, spawns,
  the System's directives). Left for a later ticket if the owner wants it.
- **The planet:** a new kind, `shroud`: black rock under slow, heavy fog,
  with a dim ember showing where the fog thins.
