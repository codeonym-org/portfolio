# 003 — Erebus is a sealed hall, and the enemies are its statues

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Taken by:** the owner (direction), the agent (choices)

The owner rejected the open plateau of decision 002 as too plain, with a
player that had no animation. He asked for a closed room with statue
monsters, in the spirit of the dungeon where the hero of Solo Leveling
awakens. This replaces the setting and the hunter of decision 002.

- **Setting:** a round, sealed hall of cut stone: textured floor and walls
  (Poly Haven, CC0), sixteen columns with torches, a door that has shut, a
  dais with a hooded colossus, statues on plinths behind the columns. The
  floor the hunter can walk shrank from a radius of 54 to 38, the obelisk
  ring from 42 to 31, so the walls are always in sight.
- **Original, not a copy:** the room, the colossus and the statues are this
  site's own design. It does not reproduce the anime's temple, its statue of
  God or its commandments, and the hunter is still not Sung Jin-Woo.
- **Hunter:** the Mixamo knight rig, bare-headed, dark with a violet blade,
  with clips for idle, run, two blows, death, getting up and ARISE.
- **Enemies:** the same rig in bare stone with a burning blade (statues that
  woke); golems for brutes and Wardens. The statues behind the columns are
  the same model frozen mid-gesture.
- **The ground shader** stays as light laid over the stone: veins, the edge
  of the ring, the burst.
- **Weight:** 3.1 MB for models and textures together.
- **Not done:** spawned enemies do not yet step down from the plinths; the
  Mixamo site was still not signed in, so no new characters came from there.
