# 002 — Erebus is drawn with models, not primitives

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Taken by:** the owner (direction), the agent (choices)

The owner saw the first slice drawn with boxes and capsules and rejected it:
the published temple looks better, though it is heavy. He asked for
downloaded assets, inspired by that temple. This replaces "no new assets" in
decision 001.

- **Hunter:** the hooded Shadow Wraith from the old temple (CC BY). It has no
  skeleton, so the hunter glides and a blade fights beside it with no hand on
  it. Still an original character: no Sung Jin-Woo model, name or likeness.
- **Knights:** the old temple's Mixamo Paladin with four clips. Red-lit, they
  are the common enemy; blue and translucent, they are the shadow soldiers,
  which get up from the ground when they rise. The plume is removed.
- **Brutes and Wardens:** an animated rock golem (CC BY), one rig at several sizes.
- **Seers (spitters):** still drawn in code: a black shard with one eye.
- **Obelisks, pillars, rubble:** three stone models (CC BY). The obelisk's
  carved texture is also its emissive map, so the glyphs light up by state.
- **Weight:** 2.2 MB for all seven files (the old hall alone was 5.3 MB),
  meshopt-compressed, loaded only inside the Erebus chunk.
- **Rejected:** a four-legged beast for the Wardens (729 bones, 4–5 MB after
  packing); a demon (no usable clips); Mixamo downloads (the browser was not
  signed in to Adobe).
- **Credits** are shown in the gate's help window and listed in `scripts/assets/README.md`.
