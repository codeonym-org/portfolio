# 010: The story ships as two tickets — all data and text, then the scene check

**Date:** 2026-11-12 (grilling, Q10; agreed: chose "Two tickets")

**Question:** how does the story split into tickets, and in which order?

**Decision:** **two tickets, in this order:**

1. **The record's data and text.** Guild entries and the guild on every project; four academic projects out and
   eight new ones in, written from the READMEs with CV dates and ranks; skills (Jaeger, Context API in, Keras
   out); the Guild Master title and the codeonym crest; the new CV file; the phone number as a contact channel and
   the agent's rule changed; everything that reads the record (plain record, `/llms.txt`, dossier, local index)
   and its tests updated.
2. **The scene check with ten projects.** Run the page, fix only what is broken or unreadable.

- **Why:** the owner's choice: fastest to ship.
- **Not chosen:** four tickets (guilds; projects; profile and contact; scene — the recommended option, easier to
  review); three tickets (guilds and projects merged).
- **Accepted cost:** the first ticket is one large change to review.
