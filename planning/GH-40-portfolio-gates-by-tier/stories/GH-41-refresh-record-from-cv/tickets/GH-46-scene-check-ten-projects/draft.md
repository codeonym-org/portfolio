# Check the scene with ten projects and fix what is broken

## Problem

The scene was laid out and checked with six projects. With ten, nothing breaks in code (every count is computed
from the list), but nobody has looked at it: ten shards on one ring, ten bound orbits around the Monolith, and
"ARISE all" taking about 26 seconds instead of 16.

## Change

Run the page with the ten projects, on desktop and phone sizes, and fix only what is broken or unreadable:
overlapping shards or labels, and an "ARISE all" that runs too long (story decision 006).

## Out of scope

New visuals, a new layout, grouping by guild in the scene: the first gate story redesigns the page.

## Done when

- Screenshots at desktop and phone width show ten project shards with no overlapping shard or label.
- With all ten risen, the bound orbits do not collide or hide the Monolith.
- "ARISE all" completes, reports 10/10, and its duration is one the owner accepts.
- Lint, type check, tests and build pass.
