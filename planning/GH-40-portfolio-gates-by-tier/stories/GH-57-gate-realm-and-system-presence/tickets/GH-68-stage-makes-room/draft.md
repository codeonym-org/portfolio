From the owner's review (story decision 003). The answer and the Monolith must never print over each other; the
answer uses the System font; the visitor can move the camera.

- Answers (`.oracle`) in Rajdhani.
- The console reports its height (`view.clear`); the rig pulls back and lifts the scene to frame the Monolith
  above it; a scrim sits behind the words.
- Free camera: tilt (vertical drag), zoom (wheel, pinch), reset (double click).

Done when no answer prints over the Monolith at 1440×900, 1920×920 and 390×844, and lint, types, tests and build
pass.
