# A one-minute brief in The Surface for hiring teams

## Problem

A recruiter must talk to the agent or open six windows to learn the basics. The owner finds the page too minimal.

## Change

A **brief** in The Surface: one window that shows, without the agent, who he is and his current role, the three
guilds with his role in each, the projects by rank, the signature skills, and the contact channels with the CV.
It opens from a visible button on arrival, from the palette and by an agent command. Every line links to the
full entry (story decision 001, point 7).

## Out of scope

New 3D models or textures; any change to The Deep.

## Done when

- On arrival, one click (or tap) opens the brief; it is readable on a phone without horizontal scroll.
- Each project and skill in the brief opens its full entry.
- The agent can open the brief, and does so for "give me the short version".
- Lint, type check, tests and build pass.
