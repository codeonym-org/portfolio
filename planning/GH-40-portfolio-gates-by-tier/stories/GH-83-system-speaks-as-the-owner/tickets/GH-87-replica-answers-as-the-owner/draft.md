# The replica: the agent answers as the owner, in text and voice

## Problem

The agent is a narrator: it talks about "the Player" in the third person and keeps the visitor at a distance. The
owner wants a stranger to feel they are interviewing him.

## Change

The text and voice agents become the owner's replica (story decisions 002, 003 and 006):

- The first reply says once that this is Ayoub's replica, answering as he would from his record. A small permanent
  label on the page says the same. After that it speaks as "I".
- It answers as in an HR interview, from the record and the interview file, and shows the matching evidence on the
  page.
- It never invents. Anything binding (a salary figure he did not write, an interview slot, an offer) is handed to
  the real Ayoub with the contact card.
- Commands are plain: "show me your projects", "walk me through your experience". The local index and the palette
  use the same wording.
- The voice uses the stock voice closest to the owner.

## Left to the ticket pass

- The wording of the disclosure and of the label, in each language the agent speaks.
- Which stock voices to compare, and how the owner picks one.
- How the proposals ("the System proposes") read in the first person.
- What the local index says when the model is unreachable, since it cannot speak as him.

## Done when

- "Tell me about yourself" gets the disclosure once, then a first-person answer from the record.
- "Why are you looking for a new role?" is answered from the interview file.
- "Can you start on the 1st at 50k?" is not agreed to; the contact card is shown.
- A question neither source answers gets "I'd rather answer that myself" and the contact card, not a guess.
- The voice gives the same answers out loud.
- Lint, type check, tests and build pass.
