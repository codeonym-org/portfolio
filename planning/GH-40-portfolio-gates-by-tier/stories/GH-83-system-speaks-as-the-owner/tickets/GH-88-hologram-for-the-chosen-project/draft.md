# The hologram: the chosen project as a holographic display with an aura

## Problem

With every project lit from the start, nothing in the scene marks the one the stranger or the agent has chosen.

## Change

The chosen project is shown as a holographic display, with an aura that sets it apart from the other shards (story
decision 005). It appears when the stranger opens a project or the agent presents one, and goes when another is
chosen or the project is closed.

## Left to the ticket pass

- What the hologram shows: the shard enlarged, a projected card, or both.
- How the aura is drawn, and its cost on a laptop GPU and on a phone.
- What shows without WebGL or with reduced motion.

## Done when

- Opening a project, by click or through the agent, shows its hologram and aura; only one project has them at a
  time.
- The frame time on the benchmark laptop GPU does not get worse than before the ticket.
- With reduced motion or without WebGL the chosen project is still clearly marked.
- Lint, type check, tests and build pass.
