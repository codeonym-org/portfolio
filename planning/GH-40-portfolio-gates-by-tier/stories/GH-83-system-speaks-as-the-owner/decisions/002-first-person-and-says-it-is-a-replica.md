# 002: The agent speaks in the first person and says once that it is the owner's replica

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (grilling, Q2; agreed: "ok" to the recommended option)

**Question:** the owner wants the stranger to feel they are "talking to me". How honest is the agent about being a
replica?

**Decision:** **first person, with one disclosure.** The agent opens by saying it is Ayoub's replica and answers as
he would, from his record. After that it speaks as "I" throughout ("I built this at OpenSNZ…"). A small permanent
label on the page says the same. "The System" stays the name of the interface (decision 001); the voice inside it is
the owner's.

- **Why:** it reads as an interview with the owner, and nobody finds out mid-conversation that they were misled.
- **Not chosen:** first person with no disclosure (a recruiter who notices stops trusting everything it said); third
  person as a representative (close to today's System, loses the interview feel).
