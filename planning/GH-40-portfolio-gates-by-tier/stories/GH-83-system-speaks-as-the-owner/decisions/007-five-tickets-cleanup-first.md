# 007: Five tickets, the cleanup before the replica

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (grilling, Q7; agreed: "ok" to the recommended option)

**Question:** how does the story split into tickets, and in what order?

**Decision:** **five tickets, in this order:**

1. **Plain names:** sectors, organisations and "stranger" in the record, the interface and the docs (decision 001).
2. **No arise, no progress:** projects lit on load; ARISE, the counter, XP, levels, quests and the sync percentage
   removed; the memory of what was seen kept (decisions 004 and 005, first half).
3. **The interview file:** a tracked part of the record, filled by a first grilling session with the owner
   (decision 003).
4. **The replica:** text and voice agents speak as the owner, disclose once, answer from the record and the
   interview file, hand binding questions to him; plain commands; the closest stock voice (decisions 002, 003, 006).
5. **The hologram:** the chosen project as a holographic display with an aura (decision 005, second half).

- **Why:** the replica's prompt and commands are written once, against the final names.
- **Not chosen:** the replica first (its prompt is rewritten when the names change under it); fewer, bigger tickets
  (harder to review and check).
