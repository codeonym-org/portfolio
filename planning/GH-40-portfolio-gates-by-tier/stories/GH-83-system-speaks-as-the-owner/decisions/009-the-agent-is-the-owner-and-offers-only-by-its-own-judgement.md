# 009: The agent is the owner, and offers only by its own judgement (replaces 002)

**Date:** 2026-11-18 (said by the owner: "i clearly said that agent is ME, so drop the replica's shit!", and of the
scripted proposals: "i said long ago that agent is the one to trigger those suggestions ..... drop those shits !")

**Question:** after 002, the agent opened every conversation with "I'm Ayoub's AI replica", a label said the same,
and the page raised offers of its own (a first-visit window offering the brief, an invitation to the interview room
by the second question, an offer on every other answer of the local index). Does that stay?

**Decision:** **no.** The agent is Ayoub: it speaks as "I" from the first reply, with no disclosure sentence, and the
interface speaks in his voice ("My business card", "What I show"). Every offer is a `propose_action` call the agent
decides on; the page raises none by itself. What the agent does not answer here (no answer on file, anything the record
does not state, anything binding) it says it would rather discuss directly and offers his business card in a window;
accepting hands the card over.

- **What stays, for honesty:** the agent never claims to be human when sincerely asked, and the line under the console
  says the answers are generated from his record and can be wrong. The local index (no model) still does not speak as
  him.
- **Not chosen:** keeping the disclosure on the first reply (the owner rejected it); scripted offers as a fallback when
  the model does not propose (the owner rejected them).
