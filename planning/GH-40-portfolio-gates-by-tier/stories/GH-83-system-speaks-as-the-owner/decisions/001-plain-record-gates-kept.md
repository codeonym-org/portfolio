# 001: The record gets plain names; gates and ranks stay as navigation

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (grilling, Q1; agreed: "ok" to the recommended option)

**Question:** the owner wants the Solo Leveling persona gone but "the theme and the system will remain". How much
of the game vocabulary survives?

**Decision:** **a plain record, with the gates kept.**

- The six sectors and everything about the owner get plain names (profile, experience, projects, skills, documents,
  contact). "Guilds" become organisations. Projects are shown, never raised: no "arise", no "shadows".
- The visitor is the **stranger**, never the hunter.
- "The System" keeps its name, its dark look, its windows and its font.
- Gates and ranks stay, only as the site's navigation between experiences (epic decision 002 stands).

- **Why:** a hiring person reads a normal record, and the Gate Realm of story GH-57 is not thrown away.
- **Not chosen:** plain everywhere, gates and ranks included (takes apart the Gate Realm and the catalog); a light
  trim of arise, shadows and hunter only (the agent would speak plainly over a page that still reads like a game).
