# 005: Projects are lit from the start; the chosen one gets a holographic display and an aura

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (grilling, Q5; agreed: "1. + homolgraphic display of selected (shosen project) aura
highlight?")

**Question:** projects are dormant shards that the visitor raises with ARISE. How do they appear now?

**Decision:** **every project shard glows from the first frame; nothing is dormant and nothing is unlocked.**
Clicking a shard, or the agent showing a project, brings it forward and opens its details. The projects panel loses
its ARISE buttons and its counter and gets a plain "Open" action.

**Added by the owner:** the project that is chosen (by the stranger or by the agent) is shown as a **holographic
display**, with an **aura** that marks it out from the others.

- **Left to the ticket pass:** what the hologram shows (the shard enlarged, a projected card, both), how the aura is
  drawn, and its cost on a laptop GPU.
- **Why:** the whole record is visible when the page loads, and the scene still has one strong gesture.
- **Not chosen:** the rise kept as the agent's gesture for every mention; projects dim until opened (the same unlock
  under another name).
