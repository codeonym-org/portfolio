# 006: A stock voice now; a clone of the owner's voice is kept as a later ticket

**Date:** 2026-11-13 (grilling, Q6; agreed: "3")

**Question:** the replica will say "I built…" out loud. Whose voice does it use?

**Decision:** **this story keeps the current voice pipeline (OpenRouter, a stock voice) and picks the stock voice
closest to the owner.** His phrasing comes from the prompt and the interview file (decision 003). A clone of his
real voice is written down as a follow-up, to decide once the replica works in text.

- **Why:** nothing new to pay for or secure now, and a stock voice matches what the disclosure says (decision 002).
- **What the clone would need, for the later decision:** recorded samples, a provider with cloning and its key
  outside OpenRouter, and an answer to the misuse risk of a copy of his voice behind a public endpoint.
- **Not chosen:** the clone in this story; ruling the clone out.
