# 001: The work is an epic, "Gates"; the C-tier gate is its first story

**Date:** 2026-11-12 (grilling, Q1; agreed: "ok" to the recommended option)

**Question:** is this one story (the C-tier gate), an epic of gates, or two unrelated stories?

**Decision:** **an epic, "Gates", whose first story is the C-tier gate.** The epic holds what every gate shares
(what a gate is, how a visitor moves between gates, the shared record and agent). The C-tier story holds only C-tier
work. The Deep S-tier gate and later gates are later stories of the same epic.

- **Why:** the owner already plans an S-tier gate and "more gates in future"; deciding the gate concept inside a
  C-tier story would put shared decisions in the wrong place.
- **Not chosen:** one C-tier-only story (fastest start, but the gate concept would have to be lifted out later);
  two unrelated stories, content refresh then gate (leaves the gate idea unplanned).
