# 010: A fifth story is added before The Deep

**Date:** 2026-11-12 (asked by the owner: "before the next planned story, add an autonomous story covering the
following aspects (do not bother me, do the grilling sessions yourself, be autonomous and track every decision
for audit and persistent history)")

**Question:** where do the Gate Realm, the System font and dialog, the persuading agent, the index wheel, the
voice pulse and the holographic map go?

**Decision:** **one new story, GH-57 "The Gate Realm and a System with presence", built before GH-44.** The
epic now has five stories (this revises decision 005). Its design is story decision 001 in
`stories/GH-57-gate-realm-and-system-presence/`, taken by the agent alone (decision 009).

- **Why one story:** the six changes share one aim (the interface should feel like the System's game) and
  share parts: the System window is used by the persuading agent; the realm renames the gates the agent names.
- **Not chosen:** adding them to GH-44 (it would delay everything behind the largest story); six loose tickets
  under the epic (no place for the shared design).
- **Decision 007 still holds:** the five uncharted planets are not gates and promise none.
