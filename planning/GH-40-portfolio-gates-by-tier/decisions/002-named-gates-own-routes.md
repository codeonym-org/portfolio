# 002: `/` opens a default gate; every gate is named, ranked and has its own route

**Date:** 2026-11-12 (grilling, Q2; agreed: "yes and gates are named! we could have multiple C tier / S tier etc
.... each has a name of course! its like a catalog of gates (pages) the user can inspect! like a PS5 game catalog")

**Question:** where does a visitor land, and how do they reach another gate?

**Decision:** **`/` opens the default gate directly (no choice to make); every other gate has its own route and
loads its own assets only when entered.** A gate is a named experience with a rank. The rank (C … S) is an
attribute, not an identity: several gates may share a rank. The gates form a **catalog** the visitor can browse and
inspect, in the manner of a console's game library.

- **Why:** a hiring team reaches the record with zero clicks and the default gate stays light; heavier gates cost
  nothing until someone enters them. Names and a catalog let gates be added without redesigning the entry.
- **Not chosen:** a gate-selection screen at `/` (a click and a choice in front of every recruiter); one page with
  depth by scrolling (both tiers in one bundle and one scene).
- **Changes the wording of 001:** "the C-tier gate" means the first gate, of rank C, not the only gate of that rank.
